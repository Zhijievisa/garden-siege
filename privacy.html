Siege Line — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Siege Line is a Power-Up for Trello published by ZHIJIEVISA PTY LTD (ABN 89 167 335 257), Suite 3, Level 6, 68 Grenfell St, Adelaide ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Summary

  • Siege Line reads your board inside your own browser to draw the battle map. The board data is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Replay is optional. If you turn it on, you give the Power-Up read-only access through Trello, and it reads this board's activity history, again only in your browser.
  • We do not run a server for this Power-Up. We do not receive, see, copy or store your board data.
  • The only thing the Power-Up saves is its own settings, and it saves them on your Trello board.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking are used by the Power-Up.

What the Power-Up reads

When you open the battle map, the Power-Up asks Trello for the board's open lists and open cards: list names, and for each card its name, list, labels, assigned members (name or username) and due date. It uses these only to draw the map and the summary table on your screen. Nothing is kept after you close the window.

Replay and read-only access

Replay rebuilds where cards were in the past. To do that it needs the board's activity history, which Trello only releases with your permission. When you click "Allow read access", Trello shows its own consent screen and issues a read-only access token for your Trello account. Trello's read permission is account-wide: it covers everything you can already see in Trello. Siege Line uses the token for one thing only: to request, from Trello, the card activity of the board you have open (cards created, moved between lists, archived and deleted, with their dates and names) for the period you choose.

The token is stored by Trello in your own private Power-Up data, where other board members cannot read it, and is held in your browser's memory while the window is open. It is sent only to Trello (api.trello.com). It is never sent to us. The activity history is used to draw the replay on your screen and is not kept after you close the window.

You can withdraw access at any time: open the Power-Up's Settings and click "Disconnect", or revoke the Power-Up under Applications in your Trello account settings. The battle map works without replay.

What the Power-Up stores, and where

The Power-Up saves three settings on the Trello board: the interface language, the number of days used for the "due soon" flag, and which zone each list belongs to (stored against Trello's list IDs). It does not write to cards, card descriptions or comments. If you use replay, your read-only token is stored as described above.

These settings are held by Trello (Atlassian) as part of your board, under your agreement with Atlassian and the Atlassian Privacy Policy. They are stored with Trello's "shared" visibility, which means anyone who can view the board in Trello can read them.

Who sees the map

Anyone who can view the board and open the Power-Up sees the same map. It shows nothing that the person could not already see on the board itself.

What we receive

Nothing from your boards and no tokens. The Power-Up runs entirely in your browser and talks only to Trello.

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems. We keep support correspondence only for as long as we need it to handle your request and for our ordinary business records, and we will delete it earlier if you ask us to, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Third parties involved in delivering the Power-Up

  • Trello (Atlassian) stores your board and the Power-Up's settings, and loads the Power-Up inside Trello. The Power-Up also loads Trello's Power-Up client library from Trello's servers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Power-Up's program files (HTML, JavaScript, CSS, images). When your browser downloads these files, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. No board data is sent to GitHub.

Deleting your data

Disable the Power-Up on the board and choose to remove its data when Trello offers; that removes the saved settings. "Disconnect" in Settings removes your replay token. Because we hold none of your board data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
