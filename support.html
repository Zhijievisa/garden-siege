Garden Siege — Help & Support

Your board as a battle map: cards are zombies, members are plants.

Getting started

  1. Click Battle map at the top of the board. It may sit inside the board's Power-Ups menu.
  2. Click Set up zones and choose a zone for each list, then Save. Until you do, zones are guessed from the list names.
  3. Read the map from left to right: the nearer a zombie is to the house, the sooner it needs you.

The zones

  • At the gate: closest to the house. Work on these first.
  • Throwers: someone is waiting on you, for example a client or an authority that has asked for something.
  • Advancing and Midfield: queued work and work in progress.
  • Dozing: on hold. These zombies are asleep and not attacking.
  • Emerging: leads and ideas that are just breaking the soil.
  • Bubbled: your part is done and you are waiting for an outcome. Shown below the field.
  • Coins: finished work with money still to collect. Shown below the field as coins.
  • Hidden: lists you do not want on the map, such as finished work.

Reading a zombie

  • Colour and badge come from the card's first label. The nine most common labels get their own colour; the rest are grey. The badge is the number in the label's name if there is one, otherwise its initials.
  • Dots under the feet are the members assigned to the card: the plants firing at it.
  • A red dashed ring means nobody is assigned.
  • An orange flag means the card is due within the number of days you set (7 by default). A red flag means it is overdue. Cards whose due date is ticked complete show no flag.

Things you can do

  • Hover over a zombie or coin to see the card's name, list, labels, members and due date.
  • Click a zombie or coin to open the card.
  • Click a plant to highlight everything that member is assigned to. Click it again to clear.
  • Click a type in the legend to highlight that label.
  • The Firepower table under the map counts each member's cards by zone.

Replay

  • Click Replay to watch the board's recent past play back: zombies appear when cards were created, walk between zones when cards were moved to another list, and leave when cards were archived.
  • The first time, click Allow read access and approve Trello's consent screen. Replay needs read-only access to the board's activity history. Each person who wants replay does this once for themselves.
  • Choose 7, 30 or 90 days. Use Play/Pause, the slider and the speed control. The line under the controls says what changed in the current step.
  • Replay shows where each card was. Labels and members are shown as they are today, due-date flags are not shown, and cards that no longer exist are grey.
  • Click Back to now to return to the live map.

Settings

Board menu → Power-Ups → Garden Siege → Settings, or Set up zones inside the map: language (English or 简体中文), the "due soon" window in days, and the zone for each list. Only people who can edit the board can change settings. If you have allowed read access for replay, Settings also shows a Disconnect button for your own account.

Frequently asked questions

Does it change my cards? No. It only reads the board, and replay access is read-only. The only thing it saves is its own settings.

Who can see the map? Anyone who can view the board. It shows nothing they could not already see.

A card is missing. Archived cards and cards in archived or Hidden lists are not drawn.

The map did not update. Click Refresh.

Replay asks me to allow access every time. Some browsers block the storage Trello uses to remember your permission inside Power-Ups. Replay still works after you allow access; you may be asked again the next time you open the map.

Replay starts later than the period I chose. Very busy boards have more history than the Power-Up loads at once (10,000 events). The replay then starts at the oldest event it loaded.

A card jumps or appears late in replay. Replay follows card creation, moves between lists, archiving and deletion. Cards on lists that were later archived, and whole lists moved between boards, are not followed.

My zombies are mostly grey. Colour follows each card's first label, and only the nine most common first labels get a colour. Put the label you care about first on the card, or use fewer distinct labels.

Does it work in the mobile apps? It is designed for Trello in a desktop browser.
